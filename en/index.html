How it works

From sign-up to meetup,
Flok in eight steps.

01 · Sign in

Sign up in seconds, start exploring.

Open an account with Apple, Google or email. Pick your interests and nearby meetups are ready for you.

02 · Discover

Find events easily.

Featured picks, recommendations and upcoming events in one feed. See how many people are going and spots left.

03 · Map

Browse the map view.

See which meetups are within walking distance. Tap a pin and the event card slides up.

04 · Join

Join with one tap.

Cover, time, place and rules on one screen. Tap Join at the bottom to apply; your ticket is ready once you're approved.

05 · Get in

Your ticket's in your pocket, check-in takes seconds.

Once you're approved, your QR ticket is ready in the app. Show it at the door, the organizer scans it, you're in.

06 · My Events

All your plans in one list.

Your next event sits at the top, with your ticket, chat and directions one tap away.

07 · Chat

Talk to everyone in one place.

Every event has its own chat. Organizers post announcements; attendees figure out where to meet.

08 · Profile

Meetups live on your profile.

Photos from events you've joined, your karma score and history — so people recognize you before you arrive.

Your own event

Flok for organizers.

01 · Create

Launch your event in minutes.

Cover, category, date, capacity and rules. Go step by step, then publish with a single tap.

02 · Publish

Live and ready to share.

Your event shows up for people nearby. Share it on WhatsApp, Instagram or with a link and watch the first applications come in.

03 · Event page

Your event page is your storefront.

Location, schedule, rules and attendees on one page. Everything your guests need to know, in one place.

04 · Manage

Approve applications, run the door.

You decide who comes; the waitlist runs itself. Scan QR codes at the door and watch capacity live.

05 · Announce

Reach every attendee with one message.

Post announcements in the event chat and answer questions. Everyone arrives with the same information.

Trust

Meet new people with peace of mind.

Behind every meetup on Flok are approved applications, a visible trust score and a profile you control.

  • Organizers decide who comes.

    For events that require approval, the organizer approves attendees. When a spot opens up, the waitlist moves on its own.

  • Karma makes trust visible.

    Every approved application, every check-in and every shared photo grows your score. You know who you're meeting before you go.

  • Report, block, relax.

    Report an inappropriate event or user with one tap and block anyone you don't want to see. Every report is reviewed.

  • Your profile, your rules.

    You choose who can see your profile. Hide photos you're tagged in from your profile whenever you want.
